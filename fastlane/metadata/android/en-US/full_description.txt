MonoLedger is the banker for your board game night. It replaces paper play money with a live, shared money ledger on every player's phone, so you can pay rent, collect salaries and check balances without counting a single bill.

One player creates a game and becomes the Banker. Everyone else joins with a 6-character code or by scanning a QR code. No sign-up, no email, no password.

PAY IN SECONDS
• Send game money to another player or to the bank with a large number pad.
• Slide to confirm, so nothing is sent by accident.
• Quick amount buttons for common bills, plus decimal amounts.
• An extra confirmation protects very large bank payouts.

EVERY BALANCE, LIVE
• All balances update on every phone as soon as a payment happens.
• A player ranking shows who is ahead.
• The shared transaction ledger records who paid whom, how much and when.

THE BANKER STAYS IN CHARGE
• Approve or decline salary requests when players pass the start space.
• Pay money out from the bank.
• Reverse a mistaken payment. The correction appears in the ledger for everyone to see.
• Pass the Banker role to another player, remove a player or end the game.

YOUR GAME, YOUR RULES
• Choose the currency symbol, the starting money and the salary amount.
• Works as a digital money tracker for property trading games, life simulation games and any tabletop game that uses play money.
• Built for a table of friends or family, 2 to 10 players.

CROWN THE WINNER
• Final standings with the winner on top.
• Game highlights: money traded, biggest earner, biggest spender and most active player.
• Past games screen with your games played, wins and results.

MADE FOR GAME NIGHT
• Dark, high-contrast design that is easy to read at the table.
• Available in English, French, Arabic (right-to-left), Spanish and German.
• Optional vibration feedback.
• No ads.

PRIVACY
MonoLedger uses an anonymous account and stores only what a game needs: the display name you type, game settings, payments and balances. The camera is used only to scan a game's QR code and is optional. You can delete your data inside the app at any time.

GOOD TO KNOW
MonoLedger needs an internet connection so all phones stay in sync. It tracks pretend money for games only: it cannot hold, send or receive real money, and it is not a banking or payment app. MonoLedger is an independent companion app and is not affiliated with any board game publisher.

Put the paper money back in the box and let MonoLedger be your game bank.
