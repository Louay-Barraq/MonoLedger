Delete your MonoLedger data

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open MonoLedger.
  2. Tap the Settings icon on the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete my data, then Delete.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous account and profile: deleted immediately.
  • Your membership in any game in progress: you leave the game (the Banker role passes to another player).
  • Your game history: your name is replaced with "Deleted player" and the entries are no longer linked to any account. We keep those anonymous entries because they are part of other players' shared ledgers.

Short-lived server logs held by our hosting provider are removed automatically within a few days.

If you no longer have the app

MonoLedger accounts are anonymous and tied to the app on your phone. If you uninstalled the app, the account can no longer be used and nothing identifies you. To ask us to remove a game's data anyway, open an issue at github.com/Louay-Barraq/MonoLedger/issues with the 6-character game code and the display name you used. We handle requests within 30 days.
