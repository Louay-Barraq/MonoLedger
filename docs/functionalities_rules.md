# MonoLedger - Functionalities & Rules

MonoLedger is a tactical board game currency manager designed to replace physical cash with a high-speed, digital "Neon Ledger."

## 🚀 Core Functionalities

### 1. Session Management
-   **Host Role**: Creates a session, defines starting capital, and sets currency denominations.
-   **Join via Code/QR**: Players join instantly by scanning or entering a short code.
-   **Live Sync**: All transactions are broadcasted to all participants in real-time.

### 2. Transaction Engine
-   **Slide to Authorize**: A high-stakes confirmation gesture to prevent accidental transfers.
-   **Multi-Denomination Selection**: Interactive "Bill Buttons" for rapid counting.
-   **Requests & Invoices**: Players can request specific amounts from others or the "Bank."

### 3. Financial Oversight
-   **Real-time Ledger**: A complete, unalterable history of all moves.
-   **Balance Visibility**: Instant view of current capital and recent changes.
-   **End-game Analytics**: Detailed summary of who earned the most and transaction statistics.

## ⚖️ Game Rules & Guidelines

### The "Bank"
-   The session host usually acts as the Bank, though the app supports a shared/automated Bank entity.
-   All transfers from the Bank must be double-confirmed if they exceed 50% of the total game liquidity.

### Denominations
-   MonoLedger supports tactical denominations: $1, $5, $10, $20, $50, $100, and $500.
-   denominations are color-coded (Gold for $500, Green for $5, etc.) to match "Tactical Kineticism" colors.

### Transaction Etiquette
-   Transactions are final once the "Slide to Pay" animation completes.
-   Disputes should be resolved via the "Detailed Ledger" audit log.

## 🛡️ User Guidelines
-   **Security**: Use session codes only with trusted players.
-   **Performance**: The app is optimized for low-latency updates; ensure a stable local connection for the best experience.
-   **Aesthetics**: Follow the "No-Line" rule—boundaries are defined by color, not borders.
