MonoLedger Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

MonoLedger is a scorekeeping app that replaces paper money in board games. It does not handle real money, and it has no ads, analytics or tracking. This page explains what the app stores, why, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • An anonymous account. When you first open the app it creates an anonymous account with a random ID. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or password.
  • Game data you enter. The display name you type when you create or join a game, each game's settings (currency symbol, starting money, GO amount), the 6-character game code, every in-game payment (amount, who paid whom, time), and final balances.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider records the IP address and time of requests in short-lived server logs, to keep the service secure and working.

What stays on your phone

  • Your language and haptics settings.
  • Camera: used only to scan a game's QR code. Images are processed on your phone and are never saved or sent anywhere. Camera access is optional; you can join with the code instead.

Who can see your data

  • Other players in the same game see your display name, balance and the game's transaction history. Nobody outside a game can read it.
  • Supabase (supabase.com) hosts the database and accounts for us, on servers in the United States. It processes the data only to run MonoLedger.
  • Google Fonts: the app downloads its fonts from Google's servers, which receive your IP address when that happens (see Google's privacy policy).

We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and we do not use it to profile you.

How long we keep it

Your account and game history are kept until you delete them. Server logs are kept for a short time by our hosting provider and then removed.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete my data. This immediately deletes your account and profile and removes you from any game you are in. In games you played with other people, your entries stay under the name "Deleted player" so their ledgers still add up; they are no longer linked to you. More options: how to delete your data.

Children

MonoLedger is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no contact details from anyone.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let players change balances only through the game's own server functions, and only within games they belong to.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

Contact
